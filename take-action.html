Take Action

Light pollution is a growing environmental issue, but there are simple things individuals and communities can do to help reduce unnecessary artificial light.

What Can You Do?

1. Turn Off Unnecessary Lights

Turn off lights when they are not being used. This is one of the easiest ways to reduce unnecessary artificial light at night.

2. Use Outdoor Lights Responsibly

Outdoor lights can be pointed downward instead of allowing light to shine into the sky. Shielded fixtures can also help keep light where it is actually needed.

3. Use Timers and Motion Sensors

Timers and motion sensors can keep outdoor lights from staying on all night when nobody needs them.

4. Spread Awareness

Talk to your friends, family, and community about light pollution. Many people do not realize that excessive artificial light can affect the night sky and wildlife.

Why Does It Matter?

Reducing light pollution can help preserve darker night skies, making it easier to see stars. It can also reduce unnecessary energy use and help protect animals that rely on natural cycles of light and darkness.

Start Small

You do not have to change everything at once. Start by turning off lights that are not needed, using outdoor lighting only when necessary, and encouraging others to do the same.
